Enludus Shorts Publisher — Architecture

Internal command-line tool. One operator (Enludus), one YouTube channel (our own). Source: github.com/enludus-daily/yt-pipeline

Operator's computer (Enludus) — nothing is hosted for other users

1. Make the video

  • Record real gameplay of our own app (headless browser)
  • Narration from our own script (local TTS)
  • Our own synthesized music/SFX
  • Compose 1080×1920 MP4 (ffmpeg)
→

2. Metadata (JSON)

  • Title, description, tags
  • Category, privacy, madeForKids
  • containsSyntheticMedia
  • Written by us per video
→

3. common/upload.py

  • OAuth 2.0 installed-app flow
  • Scope: youtube.upload only
  • Token stored locally in .cache/ (git-ignored)
  • Resumable upload; logs video ID

Google OAuth 2.0

  • Channel owner signs in once and consents
  • Access revocable at myaccount.google.com/permissions
↔

YouTube Data API v3

  • videos.insert (part=snippet,status)
  • No reads of other channels, viewers, comments or analytics
→

Our YouTube channel

  • Short published (or scheduled)
  • Operator checks it in YouTube Studio

Data stored: OAuth token + upload log (video ID, title, privacy) on the operator's computer only. No data is shared with third parties. Privacy: enludus.com/products/shorts-publisher-privacy · Terms: enludus.com/products/shorts-publisher-terms
